## 1. Review notes

**Disconfirmation tested first.** A player fires into the air near a member: if `ShotFired` lacked shooter identity, the plan's same-tick, faction-aware provocation could not work. It **did not hold**. `crates/gta_sim/src/combat/hitscan.rs` defines `ShotFired { shooter, weapon, muzzle }` and writes it before the NPC decision sets; `combat/mod.rs` schedules firing in `HealthSystems::Damage`, and `perception/mod.rs` orders NPC perception after `HealthSystems::Death`. Bevy's [message API](https://bevy.org/learn/migration-guides/0-16-to-0-17/) matches the chosen buffered signal. Keep separate `MessageReader<ShotFired>` and `MessageReader<DamageDealt>` readers for gangs.

1. **Blocking schedule cycle.** Original Step 5 configures `GangSystems.in_set(NpcSystems)`; Step 8 places `gang_death` in both `GangSystems` and `HealthSystems::Death`. `perception/mod.rs` places the entire `NpcSystems` decision chain **after** `HealthSystems::Death`. A system cannot belong to the latter set and a descendant of the former. Put `gang_death` only in `HealthSystems::Death` and `PlayingSystems`; place the perception, decision and spawn systems in `GangSystems`. Explicitly order the civilian and gang death handlers only if they share an entity (they should not). Bevy's [schedule configuration reference](https://docs.rs/bevy_ecs/0.19.1/bevy_ecs/schedule/struct.ScheduleConfig.html) confirms set membership and ordering are schedule metadata.

Plan pathfinding now; check the exact `pathfinding` 4.16.0 [crate source and manifest](https://docs.rs/crate/pathfinding/4.16.0/source/Cargo.toml) before adding it. This is a pure Rust crate, so Bevy compatibility is not an issue; its runtime cost still needs a bounded or asynchronous gate.
3. **Colour has the wrong owner.** GDD §6.3 assigns each gang a colour under `assets/gang/gangs.ron`; TASK_FINAL's resolved Q4 explicitly says weapons **and gang colours all in that file**. Original Steps 1 and 4 split weapons into `gangs.ron` and tint values into `assets/character/visual.ron`. Put each gang's tint beside its weapons in `gangs.ron`; `visual.ron` owns model paths and generic rig settings. Remove the planned duplicated `gang_tints` list and cross-file length check.
4. **The group aggro gate can pass for the wrong reason.** Original `attack_on_a_member_aggroes_the_group_within_30m` puts the player inside turf. After the hit, `GangHeat` is 120, so visible members within `sight_distance` can enter `Attack` independently in `gang_fsm`. M2 at 39 m from the player is within the proposed 40 m sight range. To prove 30 m propagation, put the shooter just outside the tested territory and hit the victim, or use a matrix-enabled non-player attacker so no player heat is set. Check the exact hit tick and precondition that the positive members are outside `shot_radius` except the victim. Keep a separate heat-on-sight gate.
5. **The named neutral matrix positive control is under-specified.** The proposed second punch follows a prior punch against a live B, but the attacker can retain a melee swing/cooldown and B's position or health can change. Reset the fixture or respawn the pair for the enabled-matrix half. Prove `DamageDealt` occurred in both halves before interpreting a lack of `Attack`.
6. **Spawner and QA liveness assumptions are brittle.** The plan's HQ post is always candidate zero, yet `spawn_ring`, camera visibility, occupancy, and ray budget can all prevent its spawn. The test's "HQ post has a group by the end" and runtime deadline must set a camera/player fixture that makes HQ eligible and verify eligibility first. A camera spinning 360° while groups only spawn hidden may starve all posts. Use controlled static view plus a separate rotation visibility check. Do not derive expected group count from a guessed 50–70 m node; inspect actual post distances.
7. **Potential presentation cleanup leak.** `src/visuals/weapons.rs` currently searches every player's descendants for a hand joint and creates `HeldGun` as a child of that joint. Extending to all loadout holders must verify despawning a gang character also removes the gun entity; include this in the client presentation gate. A stale `HeldGun.owner` can otherwise leave a visible weapon or prevent a later attachment.
8. **Resolved questions supersede original §6.** TASK_FINAL answers all four questions. Remove open alternatives from the implementation plan: outside-territory **hits** provoke, `Warn` draws/aims/walks, heat continues decaying during `Wasted`, and gang colours/weapons belong in `gangs.ron`.

## 2. Updated understanding

- `compose_sim` in `crates/gta_sim/src/lib.rs` is the one production composition function for test and city worlds. It loads strict configs and installs domain plugins. `gta_sim/Cargo.toml` currently has no `pathfinding` dependency.
- `HealthSystems` runs damage, regen, pickup, death in order. `NpcSystems` runs in `Playing` and `Wasted` (`flow/mod.rs`); `AiSystems::Perceive → Decide → PopulationSystems` follows death (`perception/mod.rs`). Weapon and melee clicks are consumed with `std::mem::take` in `combat/hitscan.rs` and `combat/melee.rs`; the FSM must set a fresh intent for each trigger. During `Wasted`, fixed ticks are slowed by `flow/wasted.rs` through `Time<Virtual>`, while the Wasted screen timer uses `Time<Real>`.
- `citygen` supplies two gang district IDs, HQ buildings, curb polygons and a sidewalk graph. `navigation/mod.rs` exposes the graph and basic steering but no path search. `population/mod.rs` resets `PopulationLoad` at the **start** of `spawn_civilians`, including early-return paths, so a gang spawner ordered afterward can spend only remaining occlusion rays.
- The weapon pickup path currently respawns every successful `WeaponPickup` after a cooldown (`combat/pickups.rs`). A dropped gun needs one-shot despawn and a lifetime. The client draws `WeaponPickup` through an observer and shows held guns only on `Player` entities today.
- Gang member presentation must use per-model GLB clips. `src/visuals/character.rs` builds one animation graph per model, and `src/visuals/civilian_gate.rs` can load real GLBs without the renderer. The gang tint value comes from `GangConfig`, while `CharacterVisualConfig` selects the gang model variants.

## 3. Revised approach

Implement a `gang/` simulation domain for territory lookup, faction relation, heat, warning, provocation, tactics and death; add gang bubble systems under `population/` and reuse existing character, combat, perception slot, pickup and presentation paths. Use buffered `ShotFired`/`DamageDealt` messages because consumers run later in the fixed tick; retain `On<Add, ...>` observers only for immediate model/slot attachment. Verify each introduced Bevy symbol against pinned 0.19.1 source before coding, including `OnTransition`, `MessageReader`, `Reflect`, `try_despawn`, `run_if` and set ordering.

Use the generator's block geometry for territory; outside curb polygons, assign the nearest block's district so roads have a stable owner. For a visible target at ≤25 m, direct seek with bounded avoidance rays; otherwise route over `SidewalkGraph` using `pathfinding` 4.16.0 A*. Recompute a route only on target-node change, blocked progress or a data-defined interval; never search the graph for every member every tick. Bound route work per tick or dispatch pure route computation to `AsyncComputeTaskPool` with entity-keyed results and stale-target rejection. Choose between those two after a seed-1 measurement; keep that choice in the implementation summary. The direct-seek cutoff, route refresh interval, stuck threshold, and avoidance range are tuning values in `assets/npc/navigation.ron`, not constants.

Keep `GangHeat` in simulation time: its 120 s is advanced by fixed ticks, including fixed ticks during `Wasted`. Explicitly test that it declines while Wasted; `Time<Virtual>` slow motion means it declines more slowly in wall-clock seconds. If the owner intended **120 real seconds**, this is the only unresolved interpretation; the task's answer only says "keeps decaying", so the fixed-time default follows gameplay timing law.

## 5. Risk areas

- **Schedule cycle and same-tick correctness:** incorrect set nesting can panic at schedule construction; message consumption and death order can make provocation one tick late. Gate the exact hit tick.
- **A* cost and wall routing:** graph search across ~579 nodes per gang per tick can exceed frame budget. Measure and bound it; a clear LOS route must avoid needless searches. A route must make progress around a real blocker.
- **Spawner starvation and density:** posts are corner-heavy, candidate visibility is strict, and HQ can be outside the ring. Measure eligible posts and in-view density during the owner route; revise placement within approved bubble rules if needed.
- **Gate false positives:** heat-on-sight can mask broken group propagation, and cooldown/health can mask a matrix control. Reset fixtures and check each causal precondition.
- **Data ownership:** gang colours in two files can drift. Store each gang's colour and weapons once in `gangs.ron`; model paths stay in `visual.ron`.
- **Death and lifecycle:** dropped pickups must not respawn forever; a member that dies on the provocation tick must still provoke its group; held-gun children must disappear with their owner.
- **Wasted time:** heat keeps decreasing through Wasted, but its 120 s is simulation time under slow motion. State that interpretation in QA evidence.
- **Owner-visible feel:** tint readability, warning, attack movement and weapon aim require an owner run; numeric gates alone do not establish the intended feel.
