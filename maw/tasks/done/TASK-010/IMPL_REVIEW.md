# IMPL_REVIEW — TASK-010

## Verdict

**NEEDS_WORK** — the core gang mechanics and headless suite pass, but the measured release scene is about 30 FPS against GDD §11's ≥60 FPS target, and the runtime "firefight" evidence is captured after the player dies. The present evidence does not establish that the new gang systems caused the frame rate.

## Disconfirmation tested first

Counterexample: an NPC's `fire_requested` or automatic `fire_held` remains set and fires every fixed tick, explaining 60 HP in roughly 0.5 s. This **did not hold** in the code: `pull` resets `trigger_left` to a roll from `combat.trigger_seconds` (`gang/behavior.rs:189-204`), `gang_fsm` pulls only at zero with the selected gun (`:394-435`), and `fire_weapons` consumes `fire_requested` with `mem::take` and enforces the weapon cooldown (`combat/hitscan.rs:155-184`). The NPC's `ActionIntent` begins with `fire_held = false` (`character/intent.rs:35-44`), and no gang system sets it.

## Confirmed correct

- The territory, hostility matrix, group provocation, heat, fixed-time AI, capped routing, group spawning, gun drops, per-model animation graphs, and held guns are implemented in their planned domains (`crates/gta_sim/src/gang/`, `navigation/mod.rs`, `population/gangs.rs`, `combat/pickups.rs`, `src/visuals/character.rs`, `src/visuals/weapons.rs`). The relevant `cargo test -p gta_sim` run completed successfully in this review; the six `gang_combat` tests also passed separately.
- A* is limited by `route_requests_per_tick` at the call site (`gang/behavior.rs:163-171`), and `RouteLoad` is reset each fixed tick (`navigation/mod.rs`). Avoidance is limited to seven rays per call and senses use AI slots (`gang/behavior.rs:301-334`); `spawn_gangs` uses the configured member and shared occlusion-ray caps (`population/gangs.rs:77-145`). There is no diff-supported unbounded firefight cost of roughly 30 ms/frame. The T9 summary shows zero route searches and rays at its final sample, though that sample is after Wasted, not during the fight.
- The lethality is possible without duplicate damage: the shipped group can contain pistol and SMG users (`assets/gang/gangs.ron`); those weapons do 25 and 12 base damage, respectively, with a 2× headshot multiplier (`assets/combat/weapons.ron:2-8`), and each bullet calls `Health::take` once (`combat/hitscan.rs:245-271`). Three members can fire close together after independent 0.5–1.1 s initial delays (`gang/behavior.rs:93-106`, `:394-435`). GDD §6.3 specifies poorer aim than police but no survival-time target. The claimed ~0.5 s is not timed by `t9.py`: its first health read follows a 0.3 s sleep, a screenshot and BRP calls (`tools/qa/scenarios/t9.py:221-240`). The 100→40 observation is a balance/owner-run concern, not evidence of an every-tick firing bug.
- The held-gun dead end has a concrete engine explanation. The project inserts `WorldAssetRoot` once per `CharacterModel` (`src/visuals/character.rs:286-315`); no project code subsequently mutates it. Bevy 0.19.1's `world_asset_spawner.rs:687-708` can respawn on `Changed<WorldAssetRoot>`, and `:354-380` destroys and recreates an instance's children on `AssetEvent::Modified`, then emits `WorldInstanceReady`. The project's ready observer rewires the new animator (`character.rs:317-372`), and `attach_held_gun` retries every Update (`weapons.rs:100-144`). Thus an early replacement is plausible and self-repairing; the evidence does not show a persistent model-recreation loop.
- The logged dead ends were checked as pointers: the revised 44.1 m HQ fixture appears in the runtime output (`scratch/qa_t9/summary.json`); the matrix gate has a positive control in `tests/gangs.rs`; the material stand-in is confined to `src/visuals/civilian_gate.rs`; and the held-gun wait is in that gate at lines 669–707. No `unsafe` occurs in the changed Rust files.

## Issues

1. **Major — performance acceptance remains unresolved.** `tools/qa/scenarios/t9.py:242-248` records about 30.2 average FPS / 33.3 ms per frame in the release T9 run (`scratch/qa_t9/summary.json`), below GDD §11's ≥60 FPS target even with only three nearby gang members. The earlier 2.4–3.9 ms measurements were sim tick times, so they do not establish full-frame headroom. A T8 regression run in the same checkout also sampled 29.8 current FPS with 40 civilians (`scratch/qa_t8/summary.json`), making a gang-specific regression unproven. The new per-tick post scan, body collection, and sort (`population/gangs.rs:77-145`) are bounded but unmeasured at this scene; A*, LOS, tracers, and animation cannot be blamed from this sample. **Suggested fix:** collect a comparable release baseline at the same HQ camera pose with gangs disabled, measure the active firefight before death, and use the GDD §11 profile trace to identify and fix the actual frame cost. Record CPU/render/GPU split and sustained FPS; do not treat the 30 FPS result as acceptable load.
2. **Major — the runtime firefight evidence is invalid.** `tools/qa/scenarios/t9.py:238-265` sleeps three seconds and labels a screenshot `firefight.png` without requiring the player to remain alive or an attacker to remain in `Attack`. The recorded `game_state` is `Wasted`, health is 0, all recorded gang states are `Idle`, and the screenshot is a gray view rather than an exchange (`scratch/qa_t9/summary.json`, `scratch/qa_t9/firefight.png`). The hard pass checks only the earlier aggro snapshot. **Suggested fix:** capture while the player is alive and the group is attacking, report the time and per-member shot counts/health changes, and mark this evidence incomplete when Wasted occurs before capture. Leave whether combat feels fair to the owner run.
3. **Minor — the held-gun gate can pass before a later re-instance.** `src/visuals/civilian_gate.rs:683-707` stops at the first `Inherited` gun, so a replacement on the following update could remove it while the gate remains green. The production retry path likely repairs it, but the eight-update wait does not prove that. **Suggested fix:** after first success, step several more updates and assert exactly one attached, correctly visible gun for each living owner on every update; assert that a second `WorldInstanceReady` reattaches it when the model asset changes. A persistent re-instance loop would also need a runtime trace, not an arbitrary wait.
