5. **Schedule order:** `HealthSystems::{Damage,Regen,Pickup,Death}` are chained (`crates/gta_sim/src/character/mod.rs:92–100`), but PLAN.md orders perception only after `Damage`; its proposed `civilian_death` in `Death` can be unordered relative to perception and decision. Add an actual damage-path headless case and a client presentation gate. Keep direct health mutation only as a focused conversion test.
7. **Performance claim:** PLAN.md's 8 ms average at 64 NPC is about ten times its own 0.756 ms baseline. It will catch a hang and some expensive quadratic work, but a cheap O(N²) scan can pass. The plan itself gives a 2.4 ms counterexample. Retain the required 64 × 640 timing output and generous hard ceiling, and add a bounded-work assertion for perception (scheduled NPCs and LOS rays per tick) plus 32/64 comparative measurements. Do not claim the mean threshold proves linear complexity. GDD §11 calls the 4 ms physics+AI and 1.5 ms AI values measurement budgets, not portable CI pass/fail limits.
8. **Test assumption:** PLAN.md says every `app.update()` is one fixed tick in the bench. The production test helper already counts `Time<Fixed>` and documents that the first update can run zero ticks (`crates/gta_sim/tests/common/mod.rs:37–80,111–121`). 9. **Scope:** Resolved Q3 accepts witnesses within 25 m fleeing instead of calling. Delete the obsolete three open questions in PLAN.md §5. The vehicle-on-sidewalk stimulus needs T14 vehicle state and should be an explicit T14 integration note, not a speculative T8 detector.

## 3. Revised approach

Build four small domain plugins in `gta_sim`: navigation owns validated sidewalk adjacency and graph steering; perception owns sliced sight/sound and short-lived stimuli; civilian owns the enum FSM and utility choice; population owns capped spawn/despawn and corpse lifetime. Gameplay runs in `FixedUpdate` under the production composition, including during `Wasted` while virtual fixed ticks still occur. Client camera, models, animation, indicator, and tint only consume sim state. Use `MessageReader` for combat signals and `On<Add, ...>` only for immediate initialization. Register reflected types used by BRP.

Use data-backed settings for every spawn distance, spawn rate, radius, timer, gait, reaction weight, model list, tint, blend, and LOS margin. The valid camera view is derived client state, not a second source of FOV tuning. A stable civilian appearance choice may be derived from a seeded sim variant or spawn ordinal, then mapped through the client model list; the sim must not load or name GLB assets. Build and cache an animation graph per configured model from that model's own clip labels. Keep player weapon layers working on male-a; the shared graph type must retain its arm masks and rest layer per model.

For first fill, publish a valid initial client view before the first Playing fixed tick, then place candidates at eligible nodes from the smaller initial radius to 120 m, outside the widened view and world-geometry LOS. Compute that initial view from the same camera geometry used by `follow_player`; refresh it after `follow_player` in `PostUpdate`. Subsequent replacements use the GDD's 60–120 m node ring. Both modes honor separation and per-tick spawn budgets; the initial budget should yield visible street life on the first Playing frame without instantiating all 40 models at once. Make lack of view an explicit pending state. The headless harness injects a synthetic view. Do not silently relax node spawning if one city seed has too few candidates.

## 5. Risk areas

- **Animation root mismatch:** silent T-pose on secondary models. The real-scene, per-model clip gate and runtime model count carry this claim.
- **First-frame pop-in:** fixed ticks can precede camera publication. Validity gating, initial fill, and an early QA screenshot are required. The exact perceived timing remains an owner-run judgment.
- **Insufficient node candidates:** a 40-person cap plus separation and view/LOS filters may leave a sparse seed with fewer eligible nodes. Measure seed-1 and several seeds; surface a real design conflict rather than silently reverting to edge-point spawning.
- **Fixed schedule and messages:** ordering errors can make shots one cycle late, and buffered messages can survive state changes. Exact-tick gates and explicit set order are required; clear any queue when a future paused state disables its reader.
- **Crowd jams and off-graph pushes:** sparse headless walkers do not cover head-on traffic or player knockback. Record these for owner visual QA and add separation/stuck handling only if an observed jam warrants it.
- **Performance:** the sim benchmark excludes skinning and rendering. BRP diagnostics plus the owner run cover client cost. The hard mean threshold catches severe regressions only; bounded-work assertions carry the algorithmic claim.
- **Corpse and indicator cleanup:** both are easy to leave behind after despawn or interruption. Test entity-child cleanup, the ray mask, and indicator lifecycle explicitly.
